From My Heart

A Letter For You 💌

💌

Click to open my letter to you

To the most wonderful woman I know,

There are moments in life when words feel too small for what the heart wants to say. This is one of those moments.

You have been my safe place in every storm, my loudest cheerleader in every victory, and my gentlest comfort in every heartbreak. I didn't earn you — I was simply, beautifully lucky to be born yours.

I see you in the way I laugh, in the care I give others, in the strength I didn't know I had until I needed it. You are in every good thing about me.

Thank you for the sleepless nights, the warm meals, the quiet sacrifices I may never fully know. Thank you for loving me even when I was difficult to love.

No distance, no time, and no words will ever be enough — but know that I carry your love with me every single day.

With all my love, always & forever 💖
